### 4.2 Verdict cells (exact, enumerated; the claim table)

Let t₁ = E1 paired t (n≈66), CI₉₀ = the realized 90% CI of mean Δ (bp/day), X = −(lower
bound of CI₉₀) rounded to 0.1 bp/day, E2 = holdout point estimate of mean Δ.

| Cell | Condition | Permitted claim (verbatim frame) | Banned |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BEATS** | t₁ ≥ +2.0 **AND** E2 ≥ 0 | "beats incumbent at matched exposure, live engine, holdout-confirmed" + Δreturn/ΔSharpe per protocol | — |
| **(downgrade)** | t₁ ≥ +2.0 AND E2 < 0 | files as the bounded-TIES cell below, with the E1/E2 disagreement printed in the same sentence | the word "beats" |
| **TIES (positive point estimate; certified not-worse-than −X bp/day)** | t₁ ∈ (0, +2.0) | "no certifiable difference; loss bounded at X bp/day (90%); point estimates favor the brain; procedure-level fold evidence t=⟨…⟩ (surrogate space)" — sub-cell: if t₁ ≥ +1.645 the phrase "one-sided-significant positive point estimate at 90%, below the BEATS bar" is additionally permitted | "real", "works", "small but positive signal"; any "positive"-effect wording below t₁ = +1.645 |
| **TIES (straddling)** | t₁ ∈ (−2.0, 0] | "no certifiable difference at available power; MDE ⟨printed⟩" | any directional wording |
| **LOSES** | t₁ ≤ −2.0 **OR** E2 90% CI entirely < 0 | "loses to incumbent at matched exposure" + the numbers | — |

Every cell's final line carries ΔSharpe, Δreturn (full-window and holdout-only), the
paired stat, and the §4.1 caveats. **Every surrogate-space number anywhere in the dossier
carries the suffix "(surrogate space)" in the same sentence — no exceptions, including
the EA gate values.** Pre-registered expectation (printed now so Phase D surprises
nobody): post-shrinkage Grinold ceiling ⇒ expected t₁ ≈ 0.3–0.7 ⇒ the modal honest
outcome is the bounded-TIES cell — a passing outcome under the packet.

### 4.3 Procedure-level fold evidence (the one admissible fold-space headline)

The pooled **unseen-fold rotation series**: concatenate each rotation champion's paired
ΔU on its genuinely held-out fold (6 folds, ~1,500 days, n_eff ≈ 300, MDE@t2 ≈ 1.2–2
bp/day), where each rotation re-derived masks/gates per §4.4.6 so the held-out fold is
unseen by genes AND pipeline. Reported as overlap-corrected HAC t, labeled
**"procedure-level evidence (surrogate space)"** — it certifies that the balancing
PROCEDURE generalizes, never that the brain beats the incumbent. Passing clause (FB1
applied): supporting-evidence status requires ≥4/6 unseen folds ΔU ≥ 0 AND pooled unseen
