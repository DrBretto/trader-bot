## Phase → role mapping

- **Phase 0:** role 1.
- **Phase A:** roles 6–10 produce blind mechanism proposals (serving the assignment, not a
  faction); roles 3–5 then draft complete systems independently, each receiving the brief +
  all mechanism proposals, no cross-reading of each other.
- **Phase B:** roles 2, 11, 12 attack all three; full-panel synthesis converges on ONE build
  candidate; bake-off criteria pre-registered in TOURNAMENT.md before any build.
- **Phase C/D:** orchestrator builds per the converged design; roles 2/11/12 review evidence
  before the report converges.
