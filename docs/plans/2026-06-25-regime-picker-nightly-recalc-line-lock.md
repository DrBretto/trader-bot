2. **The real fault is the displayed-line recompute.** The forward line
   (`> 2026-06-11`) is re-derived on every publish by `compute_canonical_dashboard_metrics`

   06-24 = $115,763, 06-25 = $116,030 vs. the corrected $117,862.)
